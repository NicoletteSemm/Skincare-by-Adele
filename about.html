HER STORY

A lifetime of thoughtful care.

Adele began her career in the beauty industry decades ago and earned her esthetician certification in 1993. After years serving the Lamorinda area, she opened her Lafayette space in 2020.

She continues to learn and refine her approach. Just as important, she listens. Adele welcomes questions, pays attention to what matters to each client, and shapes the appointment around their skin and comfort.

Her product advice is personal and honest, including recommendations beyond the small selection she keeps in the studio.
