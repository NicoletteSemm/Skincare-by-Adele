GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before your visit.

The practical details, all in one place. You are always welcome to call or text Adele.

How do I make an appointment?

Call or text Adele at 925-787-5114. Tell her the service you are interested in and a good time to reach you. Adele responds personally and confirms appointments with you.

What if I am not sure which facial to choose?

Tell Adele about your skin and what you would like help with. She can talk through the menu with you before confirming a treatment.

What are your hours?

Tuesday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The studio is closed Sunday and Monday.

How can I pay?

Cash, check, Venmo, and Zelle are accepted at the time of service. Card payments are not available.

Do you sell skin care products?

Adele offers personalized product guidance and keeps a small, changing selection in the studio. There is no online shop.

Can new clients request massage or bikini waxing?

Massage appointments and bikini waxing are limited to established clients. Adele is not taking new appointments for these services.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please provide at least 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule. Changes made with less than 24 hours notice will be charged 70% of the scheduled service.

What if I arrive late?

Late arrivals may receive a shortened service so the next appointment can begin on time, and the full scheduled price remains due. Arrivals 15 minutes or more late may need to be rescheduled and may be subject to the 70% late-cancellation fee.
